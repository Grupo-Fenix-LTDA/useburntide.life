About the company

Wholesale apparel with a sense of place.

MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA was founded in São Paulo in January 2019 as a wholesale supplier of apparel and related accessories. Our headquarters and showroom are located in Jardim Paulista, one of the city's established fashion districts.

We work with confectioners, garment producers and accessory manufacturers to curate wholesale lots that we then supply to boutiques, small chains and independent retailers. Our identity is grounded in warm, sun-worn palettes and coastal references — a design direction we call Burntide, after the copper light of a Brazilian late afternoon by the sea.

Every order we ship carries our name and our address. That accountability is not a marketing line; it is how a wholesaler stays in business in a category where trust between supplier and retailer decides who gets the next season.

Wholesale categories

What we supply to our retail partners.

Our catalog is organized around wearable, resort-friendly categories that retailers can rotate through spring and summer seasons. All lots are packaged, invoiced and shipped by MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA directly from our São Paulo headquarters.

01 / Apparel

Ready-to-wear apparel

Wholesale lots of women's and men's ready-to-wear pieces — dresses, shirts, linen sets, cover-ups and other resort-oriented garments produced under our confection registration.

02 / Accessories

Bags & travel goods

Wholesale distribution of handbags, tote bags, weekend bags and travel accessories curated to complement the apparel side of the catalog.

03 / Accessories

Footwear

Sandals, espadrilles and warm-weather footwear supplied as wholesale lots to boutiques stocking beach and resort collections.

04 / Lifestyle

Costume jewelry

Wholesale costume jewelry — bracelets, earrings and layered pieces in tones that match the Burntide palette.

05 / Lifestyle

Perfumery & cosmetics

Selected wholesale lots of perfumery and personal care items, distributed alongside apparel to full-line boutiques and small department retailers.

06 / Lifestyle

Souvenirs & artisanry

A rotating wholesale selection of Brazilian souvenirs, small artisan pieces and craft goods — a category we supply to lifestyle stores in coastal cities.

The Burntide direction

A coastal design language that runs across every wholesale lot.

Burntide is the internal name we give to the aesthetic thread that ties our wholesale catalog together — a warm, copper-into-navy palette pulled from the last hour of daylight over the Brazilian coast.

It is not a separate brand. It is the reason a retailer who buys a dress from us can also buy a bag, a pair of sandals and a bracelet, and everything reads together on the shelf. That coherence is what our buyers rely on when they plan their store's spring floor.

The domain useburntide.life is where we present this catalog to prospective retail partners. It is operated by, and belongs to, MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA.

How we work with retailers

A direct wholesale relationship, start to finish.

We do not intermediate, sub-contract or resell leads. Every step below is handled internally by our team in São Paulo, and every order is invoiced and shipped by MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA.

01

Introduction

Retailers reach out through our contact channels. We ask about the store, its city and the categories the buyer is planning to stock.

02

Catalog review

We share the current wholesale catalog, minimum order sizes and lead times. Sampling is available for qualifying accounts.

03

Order & invoice

Orders are confirmed in writing and invoiced by MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA under our CNPJ. Payment is handled by the company directly.

04

Shipment & follow-up

Lots ship from our São Paulo headquarters. Our team follows up after delivery and stays available for reorders through the season.
