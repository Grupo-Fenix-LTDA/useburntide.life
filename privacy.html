Privacy Policy

How MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 15, 2026. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the website useburntide.life, operated by MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA (CNPJ 32.487.293/0001-70).

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy is issued by MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA, a limited liability company (Sociedade Empresária Limitada) registered in Brazil under CNPJ 32.487.293/0001-70, with its registered office at R. Peixoto Gomide, 1532, Jardim Paulista, São Paulo/SP, 01.409-002, Brazil. Throughout this policy, "we", "our", "us" and "the company" refer to MAJESTE MODAS FESTA LTDA.

You can reach our team at financeiro@majeste.com.br or by telephone at +55 11 2574-6038 with any question regarding this policy or the personal information we hold about you.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need in order to answer commercial enquiries and to fulfil wholesale orders. Specifically, we may collect:

  • Identification data: your name and the name of your store or business, professional title and role.
  • Contact data: business email address, business telephone number and shipping address.
  • Commercial data: details about the products or wholesale categories you are interested in, order history and invoicing information.
  • Website usage data: non-identifying technical information such as browser type, general geographic region and pages visited, collected through standard server logs and analytics.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide only for the following purposes:

  • To reply to your enquiries about our wholesale catalog.
  • To prepare quotations, invoices and shipping documents for orders you place with us.
  • To keep you informed about the status of an ongoing order.
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax obligations under Brazilian law.
  • To improve the content and structure of our website through aggregated, non-identifying analytics.

We do not use personal data to profile visitors for third parties, and we do not resell contact lists or lead information under any circumstance.

4. Legal basis for processing (LGPD)

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados — LGPD, Law No. 13.709/2018), we process your personal information on the following legal bases: (i) execution of a contract or preliminary steps prior to a contract with you or your business; (ii) compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation to which the company is subject; (iii) our legitimate interest in operating a wholesale supply business; and (iv) your consent, when consent is the applicable basis.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the following categories of recipients, and only to the extent strictly necessary:

  • Logistics providers, in order to ship the orders you place with us.
  • Accounting and legal service providers, to the extent required for compliance with Brazilian tax, corporate and consumer legislation.
  • Public authorities, when disclosure is legally required by a competent body.

6. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as it is necessary to complete the purpose for which it was collected, and afterwards for the additional period required by Brazilian tax and commercial legislation. Analytics data collected in aggregated form is retained for standard reporting periods and is not linked to individual identifiers.

7. Security

We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect the personal data we hold, including access controls, restricted use inside the company, secure storage and periodic review of our practices. No system connected to the internet is entirely free from risk, but we treat any incident affecting personal data with the seriousness it demands and follow the notification requirements of the LGPD.

8. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you have the right to request confirmation of the existence of processing, access to your data, correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, anonymisation or deletion of data processed in breach of the law, portability of your data, information about the entities with which we have shared your data, and revocation of consent where consent is the legal basis. To exercise any of these rights, write to financeiro@majeste.com.br.

9. Cookies

Our website uses a minimal set of first-party cookies necessary to display the site correctly and to collect aggregated analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features of the site may be affected if you do so.

10. International visitors

Our website is available in English to allow retail partners outside Brazil to review our wholesale catalog. Personal data collected through the site is processed and stored in Brazil, under the protection of the LGPD.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operation or in applicable legislation. When we do, we update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Substantial changes are also communicated to active retail partners through the contact channels we hold on file.
